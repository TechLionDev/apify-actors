Hacker News Scraper API: Stories, Comments & Who Is Hiring

Hacker News scraper API: front page, Show/Ask HN, search, full comment threads and Who is hiring posts with company, role, location and salary. No login.

A first test run is usually under a cent. No login to the source site. Pay only for the results you get. Built and maintained by TechLion Dev.

Hacker News Scraper collects stories, comments and job posts from Hacker News (news.ycombinator.com) into one clean table: titles, links, points, comment counts, authors, dates and full comment text. Grab the live front page, search all of Hacker News for any word, export a full comment thread, or turn the monthly "Ask HN: Who is hiring?" thread into a job list with company, role, location, remote, salary and visa columns.

It is for founders, marketers, developer-relations teams, recruiters, job seekers, researchers and AI (artificial intelligence) agents.

It reads the official Hacker News API (application programming interface) and the public Hacker News search API. There is no browser, no login and no proxy, so runs are fast, cheap and reliable: thousands of items in a few seconds.

  • Watch for mentions of your product, company or competitors, and get only new ones on a schedule.
  • Track the front page, Show HN and Ask HN with rank, points and comment counts.
  • Export full discussions with every comment and its reply depth.
  • Build a tech job list from "Who is hiring?" with parsed company, role, location, remote, salary, visa, emails and links.
  • Feed AI tools and research with clean, plain-text stories and comments.

What data does it extract?
📰 Title, link, website and Hacker News link ⬆️ Points and comment count
👤 Author and author profile link 📅 Date (ISO 8601), weekday and age in hours
💬 Story and comment text as plain text (links kept) 🧵 Story ID and title for comments, parent ID and reply depth
🏆 Rank on the list (front page, newest, best, Ask, Show, Jobs) 🏷️ Tags (story, comment, show_hn, ask_hn, front_page)
💼 Who is hiring: company, roles, location, remote, salary 🛂 Who is hiring: visa sponsorship, emails and links

Input

Use any mix of these sources. Each one is collected separately, up to Maximum items per source.

  • Hacker News lists: top (front page), new, best, ask, show, job.
  • Search terms: any word or phrase. Choose stories, comments or both, and most relevant or newest first.
  • Story links: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=1 or just 1. Saves the story and every comment.
  • Monthly hiring thread: Who is hiring (job posts), Who wants to be hired (candidates), or Freelancer, for 1 to 24 months.

Example input:

{
    "lists": ["top", "show"],
    "searchTerms": ["supabase", "\"open source\""],
    "searchType": "all",
    "searchSort": "newest",
    "storyUrls": ["https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=1"],
    "hiringThread": "whoIsHiring",
    "hiringMonths": 1,
    "maxItemsPerSource": 500,
    "minPoints": 10,
    "since": "30 days",
    "onlyNew": true
}
Setting What it does
Search in Stories, comments, or both
Search order Most relevant (best matches, up to 1,000 per term) or Newest first (no limit, best for monitoring)
Minimum points / comments, dates, keywords, websites Free filters. Removed items are not charged
Months of hiring threads 1 = this month only; up to 24 months back
Only new items + Tracking name Save only items not seen in earlier runs

How to use it

  1. Click Try for free (an Apify account is free to create).
  2. Pick lists, add search terms or story links, or choose a hiring thread.
  3. Set Maximum items per source and any filters. Filters are free.
  4. Click Start. The default run (the current front page, 30 stories) finishes in a few seconds.
  5. Download the results from the Output tab as Excel, CSV (comma-separated values) or JSON (JavaScript Object Notation), or connect them to another tool.

Monitoring Hacker News on a schedule

Add your brand or competitor names as Search terms, choose Stories and comments and Newest first, and turn on Only new items. Then create a schedule (for example every hour). The first run saves and remembers everything it finds. After that, each run saves only new mentions, so a quiet hour costs almost nothing.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Hacker News account or an API key?

No. The Actor uses the official public Hacker News API and the public Hacker News search API. It runs on Apify's default infrastructure with no login and no proxy.

How are the "Who is hiring?" columns filled?

Most job posts start with a header like Company | Role | Location | REMOTE | Salary. The Actor reads that first line and fills company, roles, location, remote and salary, then scans the whole post for visa sponsorship, emails and links. Posts that do not follow the format still appear with their full text; some columns are then empty. Always check the text before contacting a company.

Why does relevance search stop at 1,000 results?

The Hacker News search API returns at most 1,000 results per query in relevance order. Choose Newest first to go further: the Actor then walks back in time with no limit.

Are deleted or dead items included?

No. Deleted and dead items are skipped and never charged. Points are public only for stories, so the minimum-points filter does not remove comments.

Is it legal to scrape Hacker News?

The Actor reads only public data through the official Hacker News API and the public search API that Hacker News links to. It does not log in, post or vote. You are still responsible for how you use the data (for example when contacting people from job posts), so check the rules that apply to you.
