RSS Feed Scraper: RSS, Atom, Podcasts & Full Article Text

RSS feed reader and scraper for RSS, Atom and JSON feeds, or a website address. Keyword filters, only-new items and optional full article text.

Run it on ApifyAll 23 TechLion Dev Actors$0.50 / 1,000 items

A first test run is usually under a cent. No login to the source site. Pay only for the results you get. Built and maintained by TechLion Dev.

RSS Feed Reader (an RSS feed scraper) turns any RSS, Atom or JSON Feed, including podcast feeds, into one clean table or JSON (JavaScript Object Notation) file. Paste feed links or just website addresses, and the reader finds each site's feed for you. Filter by keywords and categories, get only new items on a schedule, and add the full article text when a feed only gives a summary.

It is for news-monitoring teams, newsletter editors, podcast trackers and AI (artificial intelligence) pipelines that need RSS or Atom items in a spreadsheet or JSON.

  • News monitoring: follow dozens of sites and get only the new stories about your topics.
  • Content curation and newsletters: collect the best new posts each morning.
  • AI (artificial intelligence) and research pipelines: clean article text, ready for summaries, search or analysis.
  • Podcast tracking: episodes with audio links, lengths and images.
  • Alerts: connect new items to Slack, email or a webhook.

What data does it extract?
📰 Feed name, link and description 🏷️ Item title, link, author and categories
📅 Published and updated times 📝 Summary and full feed content (HTML and plain text)
🖼️ Image and attachments such as audio and video 🎧 Podcast episode length
🌐 Source website, word count and reading time 🆕 New item flag for scheduled runs
📄 Full article text, author, publish date and cover image (add-on) 🔢 Article word count and reading time (add-on)

Why this reader?

  • Works with website addresses: finds the feed a page advertises, or tries the usual feed addresses.
  • Every format: RSS 2.0, RSS 1.0, Atom and JSON Feed, including gzipped feeds.
  • Free filters: keywords to keep, words to skip, categories and a date. Filtered-out items are never charged.
  • Full article add-on that reads the main text of each story with plain web requests, with no browser.
  • Only new items for scheduled runs, with up to 5,000 remembered items per feed.
  • Fast: reads several feeds at the same time.

Input

{
    "feeds": ["https://hnrss.org/frontpage", "https://www.theverge.com", "https://feeds.npr.org/510289/podcast.xml"],
    "maxItemsPerFeed": 10
}

A daily AI news digest with full articles:

{
    "feeds": ["https://www.theverge.com", "https://techcrunch.com", "https://hnrss.org/frontpage"],
    "includeKeywords": ["AI", "OpenAI", "model"],
    "excludeKeywords": ["deal", "sale"],
    "fetchFullArticle": true,
    "onlyNewItems": true,
    "stateName": "ai-digest"
}

Main options:

  • Feeds: feed links or websites, items per feed, a newer-than date (for example 1 day) and whether to include the feed content.
  • Filters (free): keywords to keep, words to skip and categories.
  • Full article add-on ($): read each item's page, and how many pages to read at once.
  • New item tracking: save only items not returned before, with a memory name.

Output

One row per feed item. The Overview view shows the latest items, Full articles shows the article text, and Podcasts and media shows images, audio and lengths. Download as Excel, CSV (comma-separated values), JSON (JavaScript Object Notation), XML or HTML.

How to use it

  1. Click Try for free.
  2. Paste feed links or website addresses.
  3. Add keyword or category filters, and turn on Get the full article text if you need complete stories.
  4. Click Start. The default input finishes in a few seconds.
  5. Download the items, or schedule the run with Only new items for a daily feed.

Getting only new items on a schedule

Turn on Only return new items and schedule the run. The first run returns everything currently in the feed and remembers it. Later runs return only items added since. Use a different Memory name for each schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Why did I get fewer items than I asked for?

Most feeds only contain their latest 10 to 100 items. Older items are not in the feed.

Why is the full content missing?

Some sites put only a summary in their feed. Turn on Get the full article text to read the page itself.

Why did an article come back empty?

Some pages build their text with JavaScript or sit behind a paywall. The row then has articleError, and that article is not charged.

The reader said no feed was found.

The website does not advertise a feed, and none was found at the usual addresses. If you know the feed link, paste it directly.

Is this allowed?

Feeds are published so that other programs can read them, and the article add-on only opens public pages. You are responsible for how you use the content, including copyright when you republish it.

Can I turn an RSS feed into JSON or a spreadsheet?

Yes. Every feed item becomes one row with the same columns, which you can download as JSON (JavaScript Object Notation), CSV (comma-separated values) or Excel, or read through the Apify API.

Can I monitor news feeds for keywords?

Yes. Add your feeds, type keywords in the filter (filters are free), turn on Only items not seen in earlier runs, and schedule the run. You can send the results to Slack, email or Google Sheets with Apify's integrations.
