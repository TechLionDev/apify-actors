Google Play Reviews Scraper API: Ratings, Text & Replies

Google Play reviews scraper and Play Store reviews scraper: stars, text, dates, versions and developer replies by package or link. Free filters. No login.

A first test run is usually under a cent. No login to the source site. Pay only for the results you get. Built and maintained by TechLion Dev.

Google Play Reviews Scraper collects user reviews from any Android app on Google Play into one clean table: star ratings, review text, authors, dates, the app version the reviewer was using, helpful votes, and the developer's reply with how long it took. Paste package names, Google Play links or just app names, pick countries and a language, and press Start.

It is for app marketers, support teams, app store optimization (ASO) researchers, product managers and AI (artificial intelligence) agents that need structured Google Play reviews.

It reads the same public review data the Google Play website shows to every visitor. There is no login and no browser, so runs are fast and cheap: the default run saves 200 reviews in about 3 seconds.

  • Watch competitor apps: schedule a daily run and get only the reviews you have not seen before.
  • Find complaints or praise: keep only 1–2 star or 4–5 star reviews (filtered on Google's side, so it stays fast), or reviews that mention a feature, a crash or a competitor.
  • Measure support quality: see which reviews got a developer reply and how many hours it took.
  • Compare countries and languages with a country and language column on every row.
  • Export to Excel, CSV (comma-separated values) or JSON (JavaScript Object Notation).

What data does it extract?
🆔 Review ID, review link and full review text ⭐ Star rating (1–5) and a sentiment label (positive, neutral, negative)
👤 Author name and profile picture 📅 Date (ISO 8601), weekday and age in days
🔢 App version the reviewer was using 👍 Helpful votes (thumbs up)
💬 Developer reply, reply date and reply delay in hours 🌍 Country and language
📱 Package name, app name and Google Play link 🧑‍💻 Developer name, support email and website
📊 App average rating, rating count and review count 📦 Installs, category, content rating, price and last update

Why this scraper?

  • Half the price of the most-used Google Play review scraper: $0.05 per 1,000 reviews instead of $0.10, and every filter is free.
  • Server-side star filter. Ask for 1-star reviews and Google sends only 1-star reviews, so you do not page through thousands of 5-star reviews to find complaints.
  • Developer reply columns with the reply delay in hours, ready for support dashboards.
  • Built-in new-review tracking. Turn on Only new reviews, schedule the Actor, and later runs save only reviews you have not already collected.
  • Accepts package names, links or names. Paste com.spotify.music, a full Google Play address, or just Duolingo.
  • Keeps going when one app fails. A typo or an app that is not offered in a country is listed in the run summary, and the rest of the run carries on.

Input

The only required field is Apps: one app per line. Any of these work:

  • A package name: com.spotify.music (the part after id= in the app's Google Play address)
  • A Google Play link: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.duolingo (a gl= country in the link is added for that app)
  • An app name: WhatsApp (the Actor searches Google Play and takes the first app)

Example input:

{
    "apps": ["com.spotify.music", "https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.duolingo", "WhatsApp"],
    "countries": ["us", "gb"],
    "language": "en",
    "maxReviews": 500,
    "sort": "newest",
    "maxRating": 2,
    "since": "30 days",
    "onlyNew": true
}
Setting What it does
Countries Two-letter codes such as us, gb, de, in, br. Names such as United Kingdom also work.
Review language Language code such as en, es, de, pt, ja. Google Play returns reviews written in that language.
Maximum reviews per app per country Stops after this many saved rows for each app in each country. Filtered-out reviews don't count.
Sort Newest, most relevant (Google's default) or rating.
Filters (free) Minimum and maximum stars, keywords to keep or skip, only reviews posted since a date, only reviews with or without a developer reply.
Only new reviews After the first run, save only reviews you have not seen before.

How to use it

  1. Click Try for free (an Apify account is free to create).
  2. Paste package names or Google Play links into Apps, and choose Countries and Review language.
  3. Set Maximum reviews per app per country and any filters. Filters are free.
  4. Click Start. The default run (100 reviews from each of 2 apps) finishes in a few seconds.
  5. Download your reviews from the Output tab, or connect them to another tool (see Integrations).

Monitoring new reviews on a schedule

Turn on Only new reviews, then create a schedule (for example every morning). The first run saves and remembers every review it finds. After that, each run saves only reviews that are new since last time, so a quiet day costs almost nothing. Use a different Tracking name for each separate watch list, and combine it with Only reviews posted since (for example 3 days) and Newest sort to keep daily runs short.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find an app's package name?

Open the app on the Google Play website. The package name is the part after id= in the address, for example com.spotify.music in https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.spotify.music. You can also paste the whole link, or just the app's name.

How many reviews can I get for one app?

As many as Google Play lists for that country and language. Popular apps have hundreds of thousands. Set Maximum reviews per app per country to what you need; the Actor reads 200 reviews per request, so large runs stay quick.

Why do I only see reviews in one language?

Google Play groups reviews by language. Set Review language (for example es for Spanish) and run again to collect another language. Each row has a language column.

Why are the newest reviews a few hours old?

Google Play itself publishes new reviews with a short delay. The Actor returns exactly what Google Play shows at the moment of the run.

Is there a Google Play reviews API I can call?

Yes, through this Actor. Every run is available through the Apify API (see the code examples above), so you get a reviews API with the same columns for every app, plus filters and new-review tracking.

Do I need a Google account, an API key or a proxy?

No. The Actor reads public Google Play pages without logging in. If Google Play ever refuses direct requests, it switches to Apify residential proxy on its own, with a hard traffic cap, at no extra charge to you.

Is it legal to scrape Google Play reviews?

The Actor reads only public reviews and app information that Google Play shows to every visitor. It does not log in, post reviews or collect private account data. You are still responsible for how you use the data, especially personal data such as reviewer names, so check the rules that apply to you.
