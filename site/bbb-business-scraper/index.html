BBB Scraper: Better Business Bureau Leads, Phones & Ratings

Better Business Bureau (BBB) scraper: names, phones, addresses, BBB ratings, accreditation and categories by city and trade. Free filters. No login.

Run it on ApifyAll 23 TechLion Dev Actors$0.80 / 1,000 businesses

A first test run is usually under a cent. No login to the source site. Pay only for the results you get. Built and maintained by TechLion Dev.

BBB Scraper collects business listings from the Better Business Bureau (BBB) into one clean table: business name, phone numbers, street address, city, state, ZIP code, BBB letter rating (A+ to F), BBB Accreditation, business categories, service areas and the BBB profile link. Type a trade such as roofing, plumber or dentist, add one or more cities or ZIP codes, and press Start.

It is for sales teams, marketing agencies, lead-generation freelancers, local service marketplaces, and AI (artificial intelligence) agents that need a fresh, structured list of local businesses with phone numbers and a trust signal.

It reads the same public search data the BBB website uses for its own search page. There is no browser, no login and no proxy, so runs are fast, cheap and reliable: 50 businesses take about 3 seconds.

  • Build local lead lists: every roofer, HVAC (heating, ventilation and air conditioning) company or law firm in a city, with phone numbers.
  • Find trusted businesses: keep only BBB Accredited businesses or businesses rated A or better.
  • Cover many cities at once: every search term is run in every location, and duplicates are removed.
  • Get more than BBB's 225-result limit: Deep search repeats the search in other sort orders and keeps only new businesses.
  • Track new businesses: schedule a weekly run and save only businesses you have not seen before.
  • Export to Excel, CSV (comma-separated values), JSON (JavaScript Object Notation) or your CRM (customer relationship management) tool.

What data does it extract?
🏢 Business name and logo ☎️ Main phone plus every phone number listed
📍 Street address, city, state, ZIP, country 🗺️ Latitude and longitude
⭐ BBB letter rating (A+ to F) and numeric score ✅ BBB Accreditation (yes or no)
🏷️ Main category and all BBB categories 🚚 Service areas (counties or cities served)
🔗 BBB profile, request-a-quote and leave-a-review links 🏛️ Local BBB office and BBB IDs
⚠️ Out-of-business and charity flags 🔎 Search term, location and result position

Why this scraper?

  • Cheaper than the other BBB scrapers: $0.80 per 1,000 businesses. The most-used BBB actors charge $0.99, $4.00 or $20.00 per 1,000.
  • All filters are free. Accredited only, minimum rating, phone required, states, name and category keywords. Businesses removed by a filter are never charged.
  • Many searches in one run. List several trades and several cities; you get one combined table with no duplicates.
  • Deep search gets past the 225-result limit of a single BBB search.
  • Built-in new-business tracking for scheduled runs.
  • Keeps going when one search fails. A location BBB does not recognize is reported in the run summary and the other searches carry on.

Input

Only two fields matter for most runs: What to search for and Locations.

Example input:

{
    "searchTerms": ["roofing", "gutters"],
    "locations": ["Denver, CO", "80104", "Colorado Springs, CO"],
    "maxResultsPerSearch": 200,
    "deepSearch": false,
    "onlyAccredited": true,
    "minRating": "A",
    "requirePhone": true,
    "states": ["CO"]
}
Setting What it does
What to search for Business types or names, one per line (roofing, plumber, dentist, a company name).
Locations City and state, ZIP code, or Canadian province, one per line. Leave empty to search the whole country.
Maximum businesses per search Limit for each search term and location pair. Default 50.
Or paste BBB search links Links to BBB search result pages. Filters in the link are kept.
Sort order Best match, best rating, A to Z, Z to A, or nearest first.
Deep search Go past BBB's 225-result limit by repeating the search in other sort orders.
Country United States or Canada.
Filters (free) Accredited only, minimum BBB rating, phone required, remove out-of-business listings (on by default), states, name or category keywords.
Only new businesses Save only businesses not seen on earlier runs with the same tracking name.

The dataset has three views: Overview, Lead list (contact columns for a CRM import) and Reputation (rating, accreditation and review links). A RUN_SUMMARY record in the key-value store shows, for each search, how many businesses BBB has, how many pages were read and how many rows were saved.

How to use it

  1. Click Try for free and sign in to Apify (a free plan is enough to start).
  2. Type one or more trades in What to search for, for example roofing.
  3. Add cities, ZIP codes or provinces in Locations, for example Denver, CO.
  4. Optional: turn on Only BBB Accredited Businesses, pick a Minimum BBB rating, or turn on Deep search for big cities.
  5. Click Start. When the run finishes, open the Output tab and download Excel, CSV or JSON.
  6. To get new businesses every week, turn on Only new businesses since the last run and add a schedule.

Frequently asked questions

How do I scrape BBB without a login?

Just run this Actor. BBB business listings are public, and the Actor reads them the same way the BBB search page does. You do not need a BBB account.

Why do I only get 225 businesses per search?

BBB shows at most 15 pages of 15 results for one search. Turn on Deep search to repeat the search in other sort orders; in our test, a search for plumbers in Chicago went from 225 to more than 700 businesses. You can also split a big area into several cities or ZIP codes.

Does it get emails and websites?

BBB search results include phone numbers and addresses but not emails or website addresses. To add emails and social profiles, run a website contact details scraper on the business websites you have.

Can I get only accredited or highly rated businesses?

Yes. Turn on Only BBB Accredited Businesses and choose a Minimum BBB rating such as A. Both filters are free.

Does it work for Canada?

Yes. Choose Canada as the country and use a city and province, for example Toronto, ON.

What happens when a location is not recognized?

BBB answers an unrecognized location with unrelated businesses from everywhere, so the Actor stops that search, explains why in the run summary, and runs the rest. Nothing is charged for that search.

Is it legal to scrape BBB?

The Actor collects only public business listings that BBB shows to every visitor, and no personal account data. You are responsible for how you use the data: follow the BBB terms, your local marketing and privacy laws, and do-not-call rules before contacting businesses. If you are unsure, ask a lawyer.
