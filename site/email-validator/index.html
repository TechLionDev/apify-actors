Email Validator: Bulk Email Checker & Disposable Detection

Bulk email validator and email checker: syntax, MX records, disposable, role and free-provider checks, typo fixes and a 0-100 score. No mailbox login.

Run it on ApifyAll 23 TechLion Dev Actors$0.20 / 1,000 emails

A first test run is usually under a cent. No login to the source site. Pay only for the results you get. Built and maintained by TechLion Dev.

Email Validator checks a list of email addresses and tells you which ones are valid, risky or invalid, with a plain-English reason and a 0–100 score for each. It checks the syntax, whether the domain exists and has a mail server (MX records), and flags disposable (throwaway) addresses, role accounts (info@, sales@), free providers (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook) and typos such as gmial.com with a suggested fix.

It is for marketers cleaning a mailing list, sales teams checking scraped leads, founders validating sign-ups, and developers and AI (artificial intelligence) agents that need a quick email check.

No browser, no login, no proxy and no emails are sent: thousands of emails per minute, at $0.20 per 1,000 emails.

  • Clean a list before a campaign and protect your sender reputation by removing dead domains and typos.
  • Check leads from a contact or lead scraper (read them straight from an Apify dataset).
  • Block throwaway sign-ups from 10,000+ disposable email domains.
  • Separate personal and business emails with the free-provider flag, and shared inboxes with the role flag.
  • Fix typos with "did you mean" suggestions (john@gmial.com → john@gmail.com).

What does it check?
✅ Status: valid, risky, invalid or unknown 💬 Reason in plain English
💯 Score from 0 to 100 ✍️ Syntax (well-formed address)
🌐 Domain exists, has a mail server (MX, or A record fallback) 📮 MX records and mail provider (Google, Microsoft, Zoho...)
🗑️ Disposable / throwaway provider (10,000+ domains) 👥 Role account (info@, sales@, support@, noreply@...)
🆓 Free provider (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook, iCloud...) 🔤 Typo suggestion ("did you mean")
➕ Plus tag (jane+news@...) 🛡️ Domain SPF and DMARC records

Statuses

Status Meaning
valid Well-formed address on a domain with a working mail server, not disposable, not a role account
risky Deliverable domain but disposable, a role account, a likely typo, or DNS that fails to answer
invalid Bad syntax, the domain does not exist, it has no mail server, or it refuses email (null MX)
unknown The DNS lookup timed out; try again later

Why this validator?

  • Cheap: $0.20 per 1,000 emails, no start fee worth mentioning, and filtered-out emails are free.
  • Fast: DNS answers are cached per domain, so 10,000 emails on a few hundred domains finish in seconds.
  • Clear output: one row per email with status, reason, score and every individual check, ready for Excel or your CRM (customer relationship management) tool.
  • Works with your scrapers: give it a dataset ID and the name of the email field.

What it does not do: it does not log in to mail servers or send test emails, so it cannot confirm that a specific mailbox exists on a working domain (for example, nobody123@gmail.com is reported as valid because Gmail accepts mail). It removes the addresses that are certain to bounce or are low quality, which is most of the cleanup for scraped or user-entered lists.

Input

  • Emails: one per line. Jane Doe <jane@company.com>, comma-separated lists and mailto: links are understood. Duplicates are removed.
  • Dataset ID and Dataset email field (optional): check the emails of another Apify run, for example a contact details scraper.
  • Keep only these results (optional): for example only valid for a clean list. Filtered-out emails are not charged.

Example input:

{
    "emails": [
        "support@apify.com",
        "john.doe@gmial.com",
        "test@mailinator.com",
        "Jane Doe <jane.doe@outlook.com>",
        "info@example.com"
    ],
    "keepStatuses": ["valid", "risky"]
}

Output

You get one row per email, with the same columns on every row. The dataset has three views: Overview, Domain and DNS and Flags. A RUN_SUMMARY record has the counts by status.

How to use it

  1. Click Try for free (an Apify account is free to create).
  2. Paste your emails, or enter the dataset ID of a scraper run.
  3. Optionally choose which statuses to keep.
  4. Click Start. The sample list finishes in about 2 seconds.
  5. Download the results from the Output tab as Excel, CSV (comma-separated values) or JSON (JavaScript Object Notation), or connect them to another tool.

Frequently asked questions

Does it send emails or contact the mailbox?

No. Nothing is sent and no mail server is contacted. The Actor uses syntax rules, DNS (Domain Name System) lookups and reference lists. That is why it is fast, cheap and private.

Can it tell whether a specific mailbox exists?

Not on domains that accept mail: confirming a single mailbox requires talking to the mail server, which many providers block or answer with "accept everything". This validator removes everything that is certain to bounce (bad syntax, missing or broken domains, no mail server) and flags low-quality addresses (disposable, role, typos).

How is the score calculated?

Valid addresses on a domain with MX records start at 80; SPF and DMARC records add 5 each. Role accounts lose 15 points; disposable addresses and likely typos are capped at 15–25. Invalid addresses score 0. Use the status for decisions and the score for sorting.

Where does the disposable list come from?

From a community-maintained public list of more than 10,000 throwaway email domains, bundled with the Actor and refreshed with new versions.

Is it legal to validate emails?

Yes, checking the format of an address and public DNS records is standard practice. You remain responsible for how you collect and use email addresses (for example consent rules for marketing emails), so check the rules that apply to you.
