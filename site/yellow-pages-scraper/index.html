Yellow Pages Scraper: Business Leads, Phones & Emails

Yellow Pages and Yellowpages scraper for US business leads: names, phones, addresses, websites, categories, ratings and years in business. Optional emails.

Run it on ApifyAll 23 TechLion Dev Actors$1.00 / 1,000 businesses

A first test run is usually under a cent. No login to the source site. Pay only for the results you get. Built and maintained by TechLion Dev.

Yellow Pages Scraper turns Yellow Pages (yellowpages.com) searches into a clean list of US business leads: business names, phone numbers, full addresses, websites, categories, Yellow Pages ratings, years in business and claimed status. Turn on the details add-on to also get emails and opening hours. Type what you are looking for (for example plumber) and where (Denver, CO or a ZIP code), and press Start.

It is for sales teams, marketing agencies, lead-generation freelancers, local service businesses, researchers and AI (artificial intelligence) agents that need structured local business data.

It reads the same public search pages any visitor sees, with no login and no browser, so runs are fast and cheap.

  • Build calling and email lists of plumbers, dentists, roofers, lawyers or any other trade, in any US city or ZIP code.
  • Find businesses without a website (a great list for web design agencies).
  • Filter for quality: only businesses with a phone, a website, a minimum star rating or a minimum number of years in business.
  • Track new businesses: schedule a run and get only listings you have not seen before.
  • Export to Excel, CSV (comma-separated values) or JSON (JavaScript Object Notation), or send rows to your CRM (customer relationship management) tool.

What data does it extract?
🏢 Business name and Yellow Pages listing link 📞 Phone number
📍 Street, city, state, ZIP and full address 🌐 Website
📧 Email (details add-on, when published) 🕒 Opening hours (details add-on)
🗂️ Categories and main category ⭐ Yellow Pages rating and review count
📅 Years in business ✅ Claimed status, paid-ad flag, BBB (Better Business Bureau) and TripAdvisor ratings when shown

Why this scraper?

  • A third of the price of the most-used Yellow Pages scraper: $1 per 1,000 businesses instead of $3, and every filter is free.
  • Emails when you want them: the optional details add-on opens each listing page and adds the email and hours for $1 more per 1,000.
  • Many searches in one run: every search term is combined with every location, and duplicates across searches are removed.
  • Clean, flat columns that are the same on every row, ready for spreadsheets and CRMs.

Input

Example input:

{
    "searchTerms": ["plumber", "electrician"],
    "locations": ["Denver, CO", "Phoenix, AZ", "10001"],
    "maxResultsPerSearch": 100,
    "includeDetails": true,
    "onlyWithPhone": true
}
Setting What it does
Search terms Business types or names, one per line.
Locations City and state, ZIP code or state, one per line. Every term is searched in every location.
Yellow Pages search links Optional: paste search result links from yellowpages.com instead.
Maximum businesses per search Stops after this many saved businesses for each term and location pair (Yellow Pages shows 30 per page, up to 3,000).
Details add-on ($) Opens each listing page to add email and opening hours.
Filters (free) Only with phone, only with website, minimum stars, minimum years in business, name or category keywords.
Only new businesses After the first run, save only businesses you have not seen before.

How to use it

  1. Click Try for free (an Apify account is free to create).
  2. Type your Search terms and Locations.
  3. Set Maximum businesses per search, and turn on the details add-on if you need emails.
  4. Click Start. The default run (30 plumbers in Denver) finishes in about 4 seconds.
  5. Download your leads from the Output tab, or connect them to another tool.

Frequently asked questions

How many businesses can I get per search?

As many as Yellow Pages lists for that search, up to 3,000 per term and location pair (100 pages of 30). For bigger lists, split a city into ZIP codes.

How do I get emails?

Turn on Load listing details: email and hours ($). The Actor opens each listing page and adds the email when the business publishes one (about half of businesses in our tests) and the opening hours.

Does it work outside the United States?

Yellow Pages (yellowpages.com) covers the United States. Use US cities, states or ZIP codes.

Do I need an account, an API key or a proxy?

No. The Actor reads public pages without logging in. If Yellow Pages refuses direct requests, it retries through Apify proxy on its own, with a traffic cap, at no extra charge to you.

Is it legal to scrape Yellow Pages?

The Actor collects only business information that Yellow Pages shows publicly. It does not log in or collect private data. When you contact businesses, follow the marketing and privacy rules that apply to you (for example rules on cold email and calling).
